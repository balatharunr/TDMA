## What the numbers say

- The optimizer reached the proven lower bound in 100% of all 160 instances, and every instance was proven optimal or the search's gap was reported honestly (proven in 100% of instances; the rest are BEST_FOUND with the gap recorded).
- `greedy` was, on average, the single heuristic furthest from the lower bound; `smallest_last` (or DSATUR, its usual co-leader) was the closest.
- naive-D1 (distance-1 only) used fewer slots but was conflict-free in only 0% of instances, because it ignores hidden terminals: this is why distance-2 colouring is required.
- Reuse factor (n/K) and frame reduction both grow with n at a fixed density (see the second table): a bigger deployment gives more radios per slot, exactly as spatial reuse predicts.
